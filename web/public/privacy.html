Privacy notice / 隐私说明

Updated / 更新：2026-10-14 · 中文

This notice describes the hosted PaperLeet website at paperleet.com and its backend at app.paperleet.com.

Sign-in information

Google or Microsoft authenticates your account. PaperLeet receives an account identifier, email address and information used to verify organization membership. Our login requests only the openid, email and profile scopes. It does not request access to your email messages, contacts or files. Your provider password is handled by the provider.

Information you provide

The backend stores account and session records, profile details you save, uploaded avatars, online workspaces, code submissions, test results and community content. Personal and other accounts without access are saved on a private waitlist with their sign-in provider, email and account creation time; they cannot use member features or publish a profile while waiting. Profile fields shown on active public profiles and content you publish in the community are visible to other visitors. Online features send the relevant code and activity to our backend; browser drafts also use storage on your device.

Cookies, browser storage and operational data

We use cookies for sign-in sessions and login security, and browser storage for settings, drafts and progress. The visitor count uses a browser identifier and, when signed in, an account association in a rolling one-hour server-memory window. Network addresses and request metadata may appear in operational server logs used to run and protect the service.

Hosting and retention

GitHub Pages serves the frontend. Oracle hosts PaperLeet's backend and stored user data. Google and Microsoft process sign-in through their own services. Account and service records remain on the backend until removed through supported features or handled by PaperLeet; backups can retain earlier copies. Clearing browser storage removes local drafts and settings but does not delete server records.

Questions and account requests

Contact davidliu02k@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete account data. You can also revoke PaperLeet's sign-in authorization through your Google or Microsoft account settings; revocation does not itself delete existing PaperLeet records.

适用范围

本说明适用于 paperleet.com 在线网站及 app.paperleet.com 后端。

登录信息

Google 或 Microsoft 验证你的账号，PaperLeet 接收账号标识、邮箱及确认组织成员身份所需的信息。登录仅申请 openid、email、profile 权限，不申请读取邮件正文、联系人或文件的权限。身份提供方负责处理其账号密码。

你提供的数据

后端保存账号与会话记录、你保存的资料和头像、在线工作区、代码提交、测试结果及社区内容。个人及其他暂未获得使用资格的账号会加入私密等待名单，保存登录提供方、邮箱及账号创建时间；等待期间不能使用练习、判题与社区功能或公开个人主页。已开放账号的公开个人资料及主动发布的社区内容可被其他访客看到。在线功能会将相应代码与活动发送到后端，浏览器也会在本机保存草稿。

Cookie、本机存储与运行记录

Cookie 用于登录会话及登录安全；浏览器存储用于设置、草稿与进度。访客统计使用浏览器标识及登录后的账号关联，数据仅保存在服务器内存的一小时滚动窗口内。运行和保护服务所需的服务器日志可能记录网络地址及请求元数据。

托管和保留

GitHub Pages 托管前端，Oracle 托管 PaperLeet 后端与用户数据；Google 和 Microsoft 在各自服务中处理身份验证。账号及业务记录保留在后端，直至通过支持的功能或由 PaperLeet 处理移除；备份可能保留较早副本。清除浏览器存储会删除本机草稿与设置，不会删除服务器记录。

联系与账号请求

隐私问题及账号数据访问、更正、删除请求，请联系 davidliu02k@gmail.com。也可在 Google 或 Microsoft 账号设置中撤销 PaperLeet 的登录授权；撤销授权本身不会删除已保存的 PaperLeet 记录。
