# Few-step generation preparation

The curated ML/research preparation catalog adds a seven-module path: DDIM, Progressive Distillation, Consistency Models, Flow Matching, MeanFlow, DMD and DMD2. The two existing guides stay unchanged; five new bilingual guides supply 15 explain/implement/verify prompts. All 43 previous guides and eight previous paths retain their contents and order. Company, role, resource and interview-frequency taxonomies are unchanged. These are maintained exercise prompts, not claims that a company asked these questions.

The prompts were checked against each maintained README, implementation and full exercise tests. They distinguish amplitude coefficients from cumulative variances; a solved clean target from a teacher endpoint; boundary identity from an interior EMA target; the MeanFlow JVP with fixed r from a partial derivative; fake-minus-real scores from epsilon predictions; and separate critic/generator gradients and completed-update counters. Exercise conventions remain explicit: unweighted MeanFlow MSE, MSE paired regression in DMD, caller-supplied weights, and the DMD2 exercise's 5:1 cadence without full backward simulation. MeanFlow is trained from data rather than requiring a distillation teacher.

Primary mathematical cross-checks used [Progressive Distillation v1, Appendix G](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.00512v1), [Consistency Models v1, Sections 3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.01469v1), [MeanFlow v1, Section 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.13447v1), [DMD v1, Section 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.18828v1) and [DMD2 v1, Section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14867v1). The questions and hints describe PaperLeet's own selected teaching interfaces and tests; the small experiments do not reproduce image benchmarks.
