# Collection roadmaps

A featured collection marked `roadmap: true` in `web/src/paperCollections.ts` has a Roadmap chip after All, then a divider and its category chips, opening `#/collections/<id>?view=roadmap`; the chip takes one of the three category slots shown before Expand. Today every collection except the LLM Basics course and Self-Evolving has one. The default collection route stays the paper list and the global library has no roadmap. A collection without a roadmap shows no chip, and its `?view=roadmap` URL is rejected like any other unknown view. The roadmap is a lazy, read-only graph of 3 to 14 papers on a year-row grid (columns in half steps within 0 to 3) with exactly one curated start node, tagged Start here above it or, when arrows enter it, to its left; on phones the canvas opens scrolled so the start node is centered. Selecting a node lights its lineage: direct predecessors in cyan and followers in pink, with flowing dashes on the lit edges and a cyan-to-pink gradient border that no progress or hover state overrides. Reduced motion and forced colors remove the flowing overlay and keep the colors (Highlight in forced colors). The panel shows its title, venue, one bilingual sentence, each direct predecessor and follower with a one-sentence relation (en up to 90 characters, zh up to 40) under From and To labels in the direction colors, an arXiv card with a bundled thumbnail and its Paper page and Open PDF links stacked under the cover, and the existing Practice and Submit actions; Escape or a second click clears the selection and returns focus to the node. The panel takes up to 320px beside the graph, sticking 16px below the site header like the overview sidebar, and the full column once wrapped under it; a panel under 260px stacks the card, with the links right of the cover. Nodes show the user's own progress, while papers without an exercise are grey and light up automatically once an exercise for that paper (matched by paper id or arXiv id) is published. When nothing is selected the only text is year labels, node titles and the start tag. The graph, data and styles load only with this view. A new featured collection needs no roadmap. To add one, curate at least 3 papers in `web/src/collectionRoadmaps.ts` and mark the collection `roadmap: true`; `npm run check` fails while the flag and the data disagree, and a course never has one.

Curation is deliberately small. Every paper on a map has an arXiv version, which gives it its card and thumbnail, so papers without one are left out; among them are REINFORCE, the Policy Gradient Theorem, Netflix MF, FPMC, DSSM, YouTube DNN and Path to AMI, which is why RL starts at TRPO. A paper needs at least two independent kinds of evidence (surveys, citation data, expert or community discussion, syllabi, adoption). Each paper's metadata, inclusion and arrows are checked against the cited sources; high-confidence papers come first, then papers that start or connect a line. A node has at most 2 incoming arrows, and no arrow skips an existing path. Hand-placed rows follow announcement order within a year; papers announced together are ordered by lineage, so CLIP (2103.00020) sits above DALL-E (2102.12092): both were announced on 2021-01-05, and DALL-E reranks its samples with CLIP. Arrows never cross or pass through a node, and arrows are trimmed before papers when a layout would clutter. The evidence, sources and confidence per paper are recorded in [the research note](research/roadmap-evidence-2026-10-20.md) and its [structured record](research/roadmap-evidence-2026-10-20.json).

Thumbnails live in `web/public/roadmap-thumbnails/<arxiv-id>.webp`, made from the first PDF page with `pdftoppm -f 1 -l 1 -png -scale-to-x 168 -scale-to-y -1` (twice the 84px card cover), then WebP at quality 72 with metadata stripped, at most 16 KB each. The check requires a valid 168px-wide WebP within that size for every roadmap paper, and a failed load shows a placeholder while the links keep working.

`check-collection-roadmap.mjs` runs inside `npm run check` and covers the route contract, the geometry and clutter model, the structural data rules (3 to 14 papers, an arXiv id on every node, half-step columns at least one column apart within a row, at most 2 incoming arrows, no arrow that skips an existing path, exactly one start node whose tag stays inside the canvas and clear of every node and arrow, relation sentences within their length limits, chronology, no crossings or pass-through), roadmapPapers and thumbnail parity (one record the card accepts whole and one 168px WebP per arXiv id, nothing else), and the actual React/JSDOM render for layout, the phone start scroll (every map, centered and clamped as the model predicts), progress states, selection, lineage colors, relation sentences, the arXiv card with its links under the cover, keyboard, locale and edition behavior, and the chip row (its collapsed slot, and no chip on a course, the global library or a collection without a roadmap). Production builds also assert that the roadmap module, its styles, flow overlay and thumbnails stay out of the initial bundle and ship in a deferred chunk, next to the existing raw and gzip budgets. The evidence-based selection (two kinds of evidence, high-confidence papers first, announcement order within a year, arrows trimmed before papers) is recorded research, not a machine check. No physical browser layout, pointer, touch or screen-reader behavior, device verification, or claim that the curated selection is exhaustive or a definitive history is made.
